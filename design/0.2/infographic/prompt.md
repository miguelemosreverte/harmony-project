Use case: ui-mockup / infographic-diagram
Asset type: visual reference for an interactive HTML product book, Harmonia
Primary request: A beautifully simple, calm, explanatory top-down infographic of a home purchase across independent applications. Show a single landscape desktop screen and a small portrait mobile companion on the same design sheet. The final implementation will be semantic HTML/CSS and simple SVG connectors, not a bitmap background. Everything must be achievable with rounded rectangles, flat icons, circles, dotted connectors, and lightly raised paper-like objects.
Scene: an uncluttered canvas with two application areas: "Financing" and "Property offer". Four labelled characters/places: "Northbank", "Alice", "Ben", "Sofia". A private documents object remains in Financing. A small approval certificate crosses to Alice, and a proposal moves from Alice through Ben to Sofia. The screen depicts the moment Northbank's assessment enables Alice's proposal. Use a very restrained top-down or slightly isometric visual language, not a photorealistic city. Keep the four characters recognizable as simple HTML-friendly tokens with initials and clear names.
Composition: The infographic occupies about two thirds of the screen. One short narrative caption below: "Northbank approves. Alice can make her proposal." A slim carousel at the bottom with four chapter beats, a previous chevron and next chevron, and current position "2 / 4". Top left "Harmonia"; top right small appearance icon and "Evidence" link. The mobile companion uses the same objects rearranged vertically, with a horizontal swipeable story card. No sidebars, giant headings, dashboard grids, duplicate descriptions, disabled buttons, role selectors, code, metrics, or dense paragraphs.
Palette: near-white and soft neutral slate, dark navy text, restrained medium blue for the active handoff. Amber is reserved for a refused action; green only for completed state. No olive-green branding.
Style: precise editorial infographic, generous spacing, legible typography, gentle shadows, controlled use of light blue. Show the flow through position, connection, and one moving artifact, not through a wall of labels. A reader should understand the process before operating the interface.
Constraints: this is a reference design, not a claim of new backend behavior. Keep all text short and exactly labelled. No extra people or fifth coordinator. No invented analytics. No decorative complexity that cannot be reproduced in responsive HTML.
