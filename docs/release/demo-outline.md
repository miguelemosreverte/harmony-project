# Demonstration outline

Start the packaged `run-book` launcher and open its printed URL. Recorded playback requires Java 17 and works without a ledger. Keep the [capability matrix](../capabilities.md) available for questions about scope. Allow about fifteen minutes; live network startup is additional.

1. **Read a concrete promise.** Open Chapter 1 and its financing approval experiment. Compare the Markdown input with its independent expected result. Step through the recorded attempt and open the actual output and provenance.
2. **Compare integration paths.** Use Chapter 2 to show direct interface participation and an unchanged application behind a typed adapter. Both execute the real source choice. Explain that supported mappings are explicit, not inferred from arbitrary business code.
3. **Follow authority and privacy.** In Chapter 3, compare the bank, buyer, and unrelated participant observations. Show the limited result that enables a shared next step. Recorded perspective selection is inspection; live sessions enforce their own identity.
4. **Walk through the reference workflows.** Chapter 5 follows financing into a separate offer. Chapter 6 contrasts an approved transfer with final-leg rejection; inspect the unchanged source position and missing destination effects after rollback.
5. **Make the regression evidence concrete.** Chapter 7 shows the generated source and committed baseline. The release's binding check deliberately compiles an adapter that omits the source action; the independent golden catches the business-state divergence.
6. **Run the supported editor.** Stop any existing live network, then launch packaged `run-live`. Open the provisioned Bank and Buyer links from the printed local sessions file. In Chapter 8's order, propose a short review/approval workflow, accept as Buyer, and execute each party's eligible action. Upload the packaged legacy financing DAR or retrieve the installed participant input, then generate and download the reviewed project. Keep capability links private.
7. **Discuss extension.** Chapter 9 shows the executed bounds and competing-command result. Use the [application-team guide](adopter-guide.md) to identify one feasible next integration. Stop the live process with Ctrl-C and confirm its network has exited.

For a failed operation, show its observed diagnostic and last known state. Do not change expectations or restart with a different identity to make the demonstration appear successful. Use the recorded case if a local environment prerequisite is unavailable, identifying it clearly as recorded evidence.
