# Financing to an offer

Follow a real use case

A bank makes a private decision. A buyer carries only the signed result into a separately owned offer. This chapter follows the boundary between those applications.

## How to read this chapter

Step through evidence, the financing decision, creation of the offer, and seller review. Switch the illustrated participant view to see which information should remain private. The original describes approval and rejection; the design simulation shows the approved path.

## Current evidence and next work

The animation uses sample state. Recorded ledger evidence lives in the existing financing/offer story. The new reader will consume that recording; it must not infer an approval from an animation or client flag.
